Configuration guide

  • baseline_hourly: your rideshare net comparison.
  • minimum_hourly / preferred_hourly: ranking targets, not strict rejection thresholds.
  • locations: local venue terms. Utah and Salt Lake County cities are included by default.
  • travel_radius_miles: ranks records with manually estimated distances; routes are not calculated.
  • remote_preference: enable US/worldwide remote job discovery.
  • weekly_availability: available hours and the replacement-income baseline.
  • preferred_categories / industries: terms used in transparent relevance assessments.
  • preferred_arrangements: preferred arrangements such as contract, freelance, and part_time.
  • income_weights / network_weights: five nonnegative weights totaling 100 for each model.
  • enabled_providers: remotive and/or utah_tech_calendar.
  • income_enabled / network_enabled: switch each scout on or off.
  • interval_hours: service frequency; minimum six hours.

Automatic outreach, applications, and registration are absent from Version 1. Provider access requires no credentials. New settings rescore saved records without changing lifecycle status.
